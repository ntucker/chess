# Google Play listing

What to paste into Play Console for Opening Night. The images beside this file are drawn by
`yarn brand` (`icon-512.png`, `feature-graphic.png`); the phone screenshots in `screenshots/` come
from a release build on the Android emulator. Keep the text true to the app: change it here when a
feature it names changes, and paste it again.

## Main store listing

**App name** (30 characters at most)

> Opening Night: Chess Openings

**Short description** (80 at most)

> Learn chess openings by playing them, against an opponent who plays like people.

**Full description** (4,000 at most)

> Opening Night teaches chess openings the way you learn best: by playing them. Pick an opening and
> play your moves on the board. The guide marks the book move, shows the idea behind each move with
> arrows on the board, and answers the way real players at your level answer.
>
> PLAY, DON'T MEMORIZE
> • Explore an opening move by move, or try any move you're curious about and see why the book
> prefers another.
> • Practice a line from memory. A clean run, with no move off the book and no take-back, marks the
> line as learned.
> • Spot the traps: each opening shows the tricks the other side falls for, and the reply that
> punishes them.
>
> AN OPPONENT THAT PLAYS LIKE PEOPLE
> Your opponent is a neural network trained on millions of human games, from 1100 to 1900 rating.
> It answers with the moves players at that level actually choose, so you prepare for the games you
> will really play. Want more? Stockfish takes over at the top.
>
> OPENINGS FOR WHITE AND BLACK
> Caro-Kann, Slav, French, Sicilian and King's Indian Defences; Ruy Lopez, Italian Game, Queen's
> Gambit and London System; the Anti-London and Anti-Sicilian; and gambits like the Stafford,
> Lucchini and Boden-Kieseritzky. Every line is built from respected opening guides and checked by
> Stockfish, and each opening credits its sources.
>
> NO ACCOUNT, NO ADS
> Opening Night is free and open source. It collects no data, needs no sign-up, and works offline:
> the engines run on your phone.

## App content (Policy and programs → App content)

- **Privacy policy**: https://ntucker.github.io/chess/privacy
- **Ads**: No, the app contains no ads.
- **App access**: All functionality is available without special access.
- **Content rating**: answer the questionnaire as a board game with no violence, sexual content,
  language, drugs, gambling or user interaction. It should come out as Everyone / PEGI 3.
- **Target audience**: 13 and over. Including under-13 age groups brings in the Families policy's
  extra requirements; the app suits children, but nothing in it is made for them.
- **Data safety**: the app collects no user data and shares none. Answer "No" to "Does your app
  collect or share any of the required user data types?" The only network request is Full
  Stockfish's one-time download from GitHub Pages, which sends nothing about the user.
- **Government apps**, **Financial features**, **Health**, **News**: not applicable (No).
